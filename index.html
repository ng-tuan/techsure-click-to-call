How a claim works

For your security, we verify your identity with your phone number, order number and postal code before sharing any claim details.

Verify

Tell Sam the phone number, order number and postal code on your order.

Describe

Give the product ID (for example P-510) and a short description of the problem.

Ship it back

We email a prepaid return label within 1 business day. Write your claim number on the box.

Get it sorted

Most claims are reviewed in 3–5 business days. Replacements or refunds follow within 7 business days of arrival.

Frequently asked questions

You can also ask Sam any of these on a call. No verification is needed for general questions.

How do I start a returns claim?

Call Sam and say "new claim". After a quick identity check, Sam asks for the product ID and a short description of the problem, then gives you a claim number that starts with C.

Can I cancel my return?

Yes, at any time before the claim is refunded, completed or closed. A claim that was rejected or already completed can no longer be cancelled.

What documents do I need for a return?

Your claim number, order number, proof of purchase, and the product with all its accessories and cables. Please remove any passwords or personal data from the device first.

How long does processing take?

Most claims are reviewed within 3 to 5 business days. Once we receive the product, a replacement or refund usually takes up to 7 business days.

I gave the wrong information. How do I fix it?

Say "update claim" to add a clarification, a tracking number or new contact details. If the product ID is wrong, cancel the claim and start a new one.

Is accidental or liquid damage covered?

No. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use, not drops, cracked screens or liquid damage.
